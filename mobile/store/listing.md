## Full description (Play 4000 max; Apple 4000 max)

SCC Coach helps the coaches of a youth climbing team run practice from their phones. Everything lives in the team's own Google Sheet: no new accounts, no server, no subscription. Coaches can keep using the Sheet directly whenever they like.

PLAN THE DAY
• See today's practice at a glance: who's leading, who's coaching each group, and the full timeline.
• Build a practice from the exercise library, filtered by type and level.
• Blocks can be for the whole team, one group, or several groups at once. The plan always shows in time order.
• Adjust on the fly: nudge times by 5 minutes, reorder, or add a block mid-practice.

CLAIM YOUR GROUP
• Coaches claim the lead or a group for any day, so everyone knows who's running what.

LOG THE WORKOUT
• Tap Save workout, switch off anyone who wasn't there, change someone's group for the day, or add a quick note.
• Each athlete's profile shows their grades, goals, progress notes and every practice they attended.

EXERCISE LIBRARY
• A shared library of warm-ups, strength, power, technique and more, tagged by level, with who added each one.

SET UP YOUR TEAM
• Any number of groups, your practice days and practice time, all changeable in Team settings.
• Create a ready-to-use team Sheet in your Google Drive from the app, then share it with other coaches by email.
• Sign in with Google on any device and your team Sheet is found for you.
• Safe saving when two coaches edit at once: the app checks for changes before it writes.
• Works offline with the last copy, and an app lock keeps team data private on a shared phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your data stays in your Google account.
• Try it first with built-in demo data, no sign-in needed.

SCC Coach is free, and is made by DVS Solutions LLC. It isn't affiliated with Google.
